## 09/03
Said gives three meanings of Orientalism:
- An academic field devoted to studying "the orient"
- A binary style of thought that distinguishes East form West
- An institutional discourse through which Western powers describe. classify, govern, and claim authority over Eastern people

Subaltern calss:
- people in the lower social classes, of inferior status or tank, subordinate
- Gramsci: Subaltern classes may include peasants, workers adn other groups denied access to hegemonic power

Ideology:
- A form of social or political philosophy
- Marx and Friedrich Engels conned the term "ideology" in the 1840s to describe the dominant ideas and representations in a given social order.
- Gramsci: Ideology as the ruling ideas which functions as the "social cement," unifying and holding together the dominant social order
	- Ideology as an organizing worldview
	- It converts contingent social relation into common sense
